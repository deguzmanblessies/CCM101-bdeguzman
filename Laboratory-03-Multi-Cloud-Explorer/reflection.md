## Mission Reflection

Out of the three cloud platforms, Google Cloud Platform (GCP) impressed me the most. I liked how strong it is in AI and machine learning tools, and how well it supports Kubernetes, since Google actually created Kubernetes. The console also felt clean and easy to use compared to the others.

While researching, I noticed that AWS, Azure, and GCP are actually very similar in a lot of ways. All three offer the same basic types of services, like virtual machines, storage, databases, and identity management. They also all use a pay-as-you-go pricing system, give free credits to new users, and have data centers spread across many countries so users everywhere can access them quickly.

I also learned that choosing a cloud platform is not just about picking the most popular one. It depends on what the business actually needs. For example, a university that already uses Microsoft tools would fit better with Azure, while a company focused on artificial intelligence would benefit more from GCP. A startup with a small budget might choose AWS because of its free tier and flexible pricing.
